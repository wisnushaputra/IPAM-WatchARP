## Detailed Findings & Resolutions

### Finding 1: Em dash in document title (High)
- **Rule:** R-02 (Hard Gate)
- **Location:** `frontend/index.html:6`
- **Resolution:** Replaced em dash with colon (`WatchARP: IPAM & Live Network Scanner`).

### Finding 2: AI capsule badge on header brand (Medium)
- **Rule:** R-09 (Purpose-Gate)
- **Location:** `frontend/src/components/Header.jsx:47`
- **Resolution:** Replaced floating capsule badge and glowing icon box with a technical, high-utility network operations console header featuring a monospace `v1.0` tag.

### Finding 3: Tooltip glassmorphism (Medium)
- **Rule:** R-10 (Purpose-Gate)
- **Location:** `frontend/src/components/IpGridMap.jsx:315`
- **Resolution:** Removed `backdrop-blur-md` and `backdrop-blur-sm` layers across tooltips and modals; replaced with solid high-contrast matte surfaces (`bg-[#0e131d] border border-[#273248]`).

### Finding 4: Obvious JSX label comments in Header (Low)
- **Rule:** antislop-code (Restating the Obvious)
- **Location:** `frontend/src/components/Header.jsx`
- **Resolution:** Removed obvious label comments.

### Finding 5: Numbered workflow narration in StatCards (Low)
- **Rule:** antislop-code (Workflow Narration)
- **Location:** `frontend/src/components/StatCards.jsx`
- **Resolution:** Removed numbered comments.

### Finding 6: Empty structural labels across modals and tables (Low)
- **Rule:** antislop-code (Empty Labels)
- **Location:** `frontend/src/components/AddSubnetModal.jsx`, `EditIpModal.jsx`, `IpGridMap.jsx`, `IpTable.jsx`, `App.jsx`

### Finding 7: Numbered narration in backend services (Low)
- **Rule:** antislop-code (Workflow Narration)
